Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

1. Scope

Clip Autoposter is a personal tool operated by its author. It is not offered as a public service and has no other users. This policy describes what the tool does with data.

2. Data processed

  • TikTok access and refresh tokens. Issued by TikTok after the account owner authorizes the application through Login Kit. Used only to call the TikTok Content Posting API on behalf of that same account.
  • Basic TikTok profile information returned by the user.info.basic scope (open id, display name, avatar). Used to confirm which account content will be posted to.
  • Video files and captions supplied by the account owner for publication.

3. Storage

Tokens are stored locally on the operator's own machine, in a file that is not published or shared. Video files stay on that machine until they are uploaded to TikTok. No database, analytics service or third-party processor is used.

4. What is not done

  • Data is never sold, rented or shared with third parties.
  • No advertising or tracking technologies are used.
  • No data belonging to other TikTok users is collected.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the tool is in use and are deleted when authorization is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings, under Security and login, Manage app permissions. Deleting the local token file also ends all access.

6. Third-party services

The only third-party service used is TikTok. Data sent to TikTok is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through github.com/rainall27.
